The core loop

How it works

Five steps from signup to a streak that sticks. The entire interaction happens inside your SMS app — no download required.

1

Sign up

Create your account with a phone number, email, or Google. Enter your phone number — that's where reminders land.

2

Choose your habits

Pick from templates (workout, meditation, water, reading, sleep) or create a fully custom habit with a name, type, and unit.

3

Set your reminder time

Choose what time each day you want to be reminded. All times are in your local timezone — change it anytime in Settings.

4

Reply to log

At your chosen time, you get a text. Reply Y to mark it done, N to skip, or a number for tracking habits (e.g. "8" for 8 glasses of water).

5

Watch your streak grow

Every completion extends your streak. Hit 7, 30, or 100 days and you'll get a celebration message. The dashboard shows your full history.

Common questions

FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask before signing up.

Does it work internationally?

Yes. SMS reminders work in India (via Twilio and MSG91 for lower costs) and the US. Other countries are supported via Twilio's global network. WhatsApp is available anywhere you have data.

What if I miss a day?

Your streak resets to zero. But you get one grace day per month — text GRACE the next morning and HabitSMS will restore any streak that was broken yesterday. You can only use it once per calendar month.

Can I track multiple habits?

Yes. Starter supports 3 active habits; Pro and Team support up to 50. Each habit gets its own reminder time and tracking history. When you reply, the oldest unanswered habit for the day is logged first.

What if I want to take a break?

Text PAUSE to stop all reminders immediately. Text RESUME when you're back. Your streak won't keep running while paused — but it also won't reset. It picks up from where you left off.

How do I get an invoice?

Go to Settings → Billing History. Every successful payment is listed there with a Download Invoice button. PDFs are generated by Dodo Payments and contain full billing details.

What does the number in the plan price buy me exactly?

Each active habit = one daily SMS reminder + streak tracking + participation in the daily recap. You can have up to the plan limit active at once. Inactive (paused) habits don't count toward the limit.

Can I change my reminder time?

Yes — edit any habit from the dashboard and change the reminder time. The next reminder will go out at the new time. Timezone is set per-account in Settings and applies to all your habits.
